Live state (LIVE / SLATE / SLOW / STALLED / OFF) is read from the server's own HLS output every 5s — it answers "is this channel putting segments out right now". Picture and sound detail comes from the synthetic viewer, which pulls a segment through the CDN and decodes it exactly as a viewer receives it; a full pass over all channels takes ~4.5 min, so that line is minutes old by design. SLATE means the channel is producing but showing the standby card, not the programme.
